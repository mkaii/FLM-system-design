Booking — what it does and doesn't own

Booking holds status (PENDING → CONFIRMED / CANCELLED / EXPIRED), a totalAmount computed from its ShowSeats via PricingStrategy, and the User who made it. It does not hold its own copy of seat numbers or show times — ShowSeat is the single source of truth for that, and Booking only ever points at it. If a refund policy later needs "which seats, which show, what price were locked in at booking time," that data is derivable from the referenced ShowSeats and the PricingStrategy that ran — not duplicated onto Booking itself.
